## 3. T1/T2 Cautionary Patterns & Anti-Patterns to Avoid

| Short Form | Venue | Issue Observed | Better Direction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`POMMM`** | *Nat. Photon.* 2025 | Triple-M stutter (`P-O-M-M-M`, 5 spoken syllables) | Fuse into a 2-syllable word or 3-letter initialism |
| **`OAM-MDNN`** | *Nat. Photon.* 2026 | 8 characters + hyphen, 7 spoken syllables in talks | Name the architecture in 1-2 syllables; explain OAM multiplexing in the subtitle/abstract |
| **`PDNN`** (and drafts like `OMDNN`) | *Nature* 2022 | 4-5 consonant-only letters; phonetically confused with `DNN`, `D2NN`, `DONN`, `PDNN` | Replace stacked `D/N` initialisms with a pronounceable backronym or portmanteau |
| **`D2NN`** / **`DN2`** | *Science* 2018 / *Nat. Photon.* 2025 | Superscript `D^2NN` breaks plain text, BibTeX, and speech; `DN2` collides phonetically with `D2NN` | Keep pure ASCII letters without digits or superscripts |
| **`PIN`** / **`OEM`** | *Nat. Comput. Sci.* 2025 / *Nat. Nanotechnol.* 2024 | Short and clean, but shares exact letters with entrenched terms (`p-i-n` diode, industrial `OEM`) | Always run `tech_naming_tool.py check` before locking a 3-letter word |
