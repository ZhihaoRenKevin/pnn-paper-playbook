## 2. Top-3 Verified Measured SOTA Highlights per Platform (`77/77 Cell-by-Cell Verified`)

1. **`MZI` (Coherent Mach–Zehnder Interferometer Meshes & Transceivers)**:
   - **Throughput, Channel Scale & Transit Latency**: `65.5 TOPS` sustained (`ABFP16`, `78 W` electrical + `1.6 W` optical, `system-level`), `128` photonic vector units per PTC across `four 128 × 128 PTCs` (`chip-level`), and `200 ps` optical time-of-flight latency (`core-only`) (`Nature_25V640_368`, p.1, SI p.10–11).
   - **Optical Energy Sensitivity & Modulation Rate**: `40 aJ/MAC` (`<1 photon/multiply` at `98.8%` MNIST accuracy, `core-only`) and `50 Gbps/MZM` (`2.4 Tbps` total across `48 MZMs`, `chip-level`) (`Science_22V378_270`, p.1–2).
   - **Phase Setting Resolution & System Efficiency**: `16-bit` optical phase setting (`30 dB` photodetection dynamic range, `device-level`, `Nature Photonics_17V11_441`, p.4) and `8.19 TOPS` at `4.21 TOPS/W` (`chip-level`) / `2.38 TOPS/W` (`system-level`) (`Nature_25V640_361`, p.2).
2. **`MRR` (Microring Resonator Weight Banks & Microcomb Convolutional Accelerators)**:
   - **Throughput & WDM Parallelism**: `11.321 TOPS` vector convolutional speed across `10` parallel kernels (`core-only`), `90` soliton crystal microcomb wavelengths (`36 nm` C-band), and `62.9 GBaud` input data rate (`system-level`) (`Nature_21V589_44`, p.1–2, p.9).
   - **On-Chip Latency, Compute Density & Energy**: `570 ps` end-to-end chip delay (`46 ps` single-layer core computation time), `3.5 TOPS/mm²` compute density (`core-only`, `0.6 mm²`), and `345 fJ/OP` (`core-only`) on a 3-layer SOI PDNN (`Nature_22V606_501`, p.7–9); up to `34.04 TOPS/mm²` in multidomain MRR tensor cores (`Light Science & Applications_25V14_27`, p.1).
   - **Weight Control Precision**: `8-bit` continuous multichannel MRR weight bank accuracy (`device-level`, `Nature Electronics_21V4_837`, p.4).
3. **`PCM` (Non-Volatile Phase-Change Material Synaptic Crossbars)**:
   - **Compute Density, Wavelength Channels & Latency**: `1.2 TOPS/mm²` (`SiN` `9 × 4` core at `12 GHz`, p.10) and `162 TOPS/mm²` (`SOI` `8 × 8` matrix at `18 GHz`, SI p.19) (`core-only`), `5-bit` (`σ = 0.008`) system resolution, `16` microcomb wavelengths (`λ1` to `λ16`), and `8.1 µs/image` processing time at `2 GHz` (`13.5 GHz` `2 × 1` MVM eye diagram) (`Nature_21V589_52`, p.6, p.9–10, SI p.19).
   - **Partially Coherent Parallelism**: `0.108 TOPS` (`core-only`, `2 GSa/s` data-loading rate) and `4-bit` precision on a `3 × 3` PCM photonic core (`Nature_24V632_55`, p.2, p.4–5).
4. **`TFLN` (Thin-Film Lithium Niobate Electro-Optic Processors)**:
   - **Symbol Rate & Core Throughput**: `106 GBaud` symbol rate (`core-only`), `212 GOPS` single-wavelength speed (`core-only`), and `3.3-bit` effective precision at `106 GBaud` (`4.5-bit` at `32 GBaud`) on a programmable TFLN Hopfield-Ising processor (`Nature_25V648_576`, p.4).
   - **Compute Density & Image Edge-Detection Latency**: `17.5 GOPS/mm²` chip area density (`M = 7` WDM channels, `A2 = 8 mm²` per TFLN modulator, `Science Advances_25V11_eadu0228`, p.8–9) and `244 ns` total computation time for a `250 × 250` image at `256 GSa/s` (`core-only`, `Nature_24V627_80`, p.6).
5. **`diffractive` (Free-Space & Integrated Diffractive Neural Networks)**:
   - **All-Analog Photoelectronic Speed & Efficiency**: `4.55 × 10^3 TOPS` (`4.55 POPS` system-level speed), `7.48 × 10^4 TOPS/W` (`74.8 POPS/W` systemic energy efficiency), and `72 ns` (`3 pulses × 24 ns` at `500 MHz` clock) latency for 3-class ImageNet (`Nature_23V623_48`, p.8–9).
   - **Integrated Diffractive Compute Density**: `447.7 TOPS/mm²` core computing density (`217.6 TOPS` throughput, `7.28 TOPS/W` system efficiency, `30.2 ps` optical latency) on the CMOS-compatible TDONN chip (`Nature Communications_24V15_6189`, p.1, p.8).
   - **Reconfigurable Optoelectronic Neuron Scale**: `2.20 million` optoelectronic neurons (`D-NIN-1`; `1.47 million` in `D-RNN`, `0.94 million` in `D2NN`) with `8-bit` SLM phase modulation and `16-bit` sCMOS detection (`Nature Photonics_21V15_367`, p.5, p.8).
6. **`metasurface` (Subwavelength Nanophotonic Metasurface Processors)**:
   - **Generative Synthesis Speed, Density & Efficiency**: `3.57 × 10^4 TOPS` (`138 kHz` generator frame rate) / `2.49 × 10^2 TOPS` (`480 Hz` full end-to-end rate), `2.62 × 10^2 TOPS/mm²` (`136.5 mm²` footprint), and `6.64 × 10^2 TOPS/W` (`53.85 W` total power) on the LightGen chip (`Science_25V390_1259`, SI p.10–11).
   - **Edge Vision Meta-Atom Scale & Transit Time**: `41 million` optical metasurface front-end parameters (`87,000` digital back-end parameters), `2 ps` deployable prototype optical latency (`1.67 ns` 50-cm lab testbed), and `0.69 ms` 3-task system latency (`Nature_26V654_917`, p.1, SI p.52).
7. **`reservoir` (Passive Waveguide, Time-Delay & Polarization Photonic Reservoirs)**:
   - **Ultrawideband WDM Throughput, Baud Rate & Latency**: `1.6 Tbit/s` aggregate WDM throughput (`8 channels × 100 GBaud`), `112 GBaud` maximum single-lane symbol rate, `55 ps` optical latency, and `67.5 fJ/bit` (`~108 mW`) microheater tuning energy (`Science_26V392_eady5344`, p.1–2, p.4–5, p.9–10).

9. **`Ising` (Coherent DOPO, Analog Optical Computer & Recurrent Photonic Ising Machines)**:

10. **`in-sensor` (Photodiode & 2D Semiconductor In-Sensor Computing Processors)**:
   - **Single-Device Photodetector Energy & Area Efficiency**: `2.24 pJ/MAC` energy consumption and `32 TOPS/mm²` area efficiency (`3.21 GOPS` throughput) in a single-device InGaAs photodetector LinExp-$\tau$ MAC unit (`Nature Communications_26V17_Single-device`, p.2, p.6).
