## 1. The Five Mandatory Hardware Ablation Controls (Pre-Empting `C5`, `C8`, `C9`)

Across *Nature*, *Science*, *Nature Photonics*, *Nature Electronics*, and *Nature Communications* peer-review files, the single most common ML/system rejection reason is:
> In paraphrase: is the photonic chip doing useful computation, or are the digital preprocessing and the digital linear readout doing most of the work?

Every experimental PNN paper **must include at least four of the following five ablation controls** directly in a main figure panel (typically `Fig. 3` or `Fig. 4`) or an explicitly referenced Supplementary Table:

| Control ID | Control Name | Exact Experimental / Numerical Procedure | What It Proves to Referees | Canonical In-Library Exemplars |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Control 1** | **Input-Bypass / Bias-Only Digital Readout Baseline** | Feed the exact same preprocessed input features $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^{D}$ directly into a digital linear classifier $\mathbf{y} = \mathbf{W}_{\text{dig}}\mathbf{x} + \mathbf{b}$ (or train only the output biases $\mathbf{b}$ with optical inputs blocked), using the **identical optimizer, learning rate, step budget, and train/val/test split**. | Proves whether the physical optical transformation $\mathbf{\Phi}(\mathbf{x}; \mathbf{V})$ expands separability or whether the dataset is already linearly separable in the preprocessed feature space. | `Nature_22V601_549` (Wright PAT identity replacement); `Nature_25V640_361` (Hua PACE laser-off control) |
| **Control 2** | **Untrained Core (`V = 0`) vs. In-Situ Trained Core (`V = V*`)** | Evaluate two hardware regimes: **(a) Untrained Core** ($\mathbf{V} = \mathbf{0}$ or fixed random voltages, acting as a static random projection / reservoir where only the digital readout layer is fit) vs. **(b) Hardware-in-the-Loop Optimized Core** ($\mathbf{V} \to \mathbf{V}^*$ updated via in-situ BP, PAT, FFM, or SPSA). | The accuracy gain $\Delta\text{Acc}_{\text{core}} = \text{Acc}(\mathbf{V}^*) - \text{Acc}(\mathbf{V}=\mathbf{0})$ and loss drop $\Delta\mathcal{L}_{\text{core}}$ isolate the exact physical contribution of programmable optical tuning. | `Nature Communications_22V13_1044` (Zhu diffractive chip); `Nature_24V632_280` (Xue FFM) |
| **Control 3** | **Parameter- & Precision-Matched Digital Baseline** | Construct a digital model with the **exact same number of trainable degrees of freedom** ($K_{\text{opt}} + K_{\text{readout}}$) quantized to the measured hardware effective bit precision ($\text{ENOB}$ or weight bit levels, plus measured detector Gaussian noise $\sigma_{\text{det}}$). | Pre-empts `C8` (*"unfair comparison against an under-parameterized or un-tuned digital model"*). | `Nature_21V589_52` (Feldmann PTC); `Nature_25V640_368` (Ahmed Lightmatter) |
| **Control 4** | **Physical Mechanism Isolation Ablation** | Isolate the claimed physical mechanism from co-existing background effects under identical drive conditions:<br>• **Electro-Optic (Pockels / Kerr) vs. Thermo-Optic Self-Heating**: Measure frequency response above thermal cutoff ($>1\text{ MHz}$) or verify zero DC current $I\text{–}V$ leakage.<br>• **Non-Volatile PCM State vs. Volatile Thermal Transient**: Measure optical transmission minutes/hours after pulse termination at zero holding voltage. | Prevents referees (`C11`/`C14`) from attributing task performance to parasitic thermal drift. | `Nature_24V632_55` (Dong partial coherence vs full coherence); `Nature Photonics_21V15_541` (Sb₂Se₃ PCM) |
| **Control 5** | **Frozen-Checkpoint Multi-Readout Stability & Drift Test** | Lock the trained optimal voltage/phase vector $\mathbf{V}^*$ and re-run inference across the test samples $R \ge 3\text{–}10$ times over minutes/hours without re-optimizing any parameter. Report mean $\pm$ s.d. or exact per-run sample counts ($k/n$). | Proves that the reported test score is a reproducible physical state rather than a single noise fluctuation. | `Nature_22V606_501` (Ashtiani PDNN); `Nature_25V645_354` (Kalinin AOC) |

## 2. Dataset Selection Ladder by Target Journal Tier (`C7` Pre-Emption)

Referees at `T1–T3` routinely reject or force major revisions on manuscripts that rely solely on toy benchmarks (`Iris`, 3-class `Digits`, or `XOR`) without either a harder dataset or a real-world physical signal demonstration.

| Target Tier | Minimum Acceptable Benchmark Portfolio | How to Handle Small-DOF Chips ($N_{\text{in}} = 4\text{–}16$ ports, $M = 10\text{–}64$ controls) Legitimately |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1 (`T1`)**<br>*Nature*, *Science* | **3–4 distinct task families**:<br>1. Standard vision benchmark (`MNIST` + `Fashion-MNIST` or `CIFAR-10` / `ImageNet` subset) at $\ge 1,000$ test samples.<br>2. High-dimensional or generative/recurrent task (e.g., image reconstruction, dynamical PDE/Ising solving, audio/speech recognition, or real-time fiber/wireless equalization).<br>3. Hardware-in-the-loop perturbation recovery demo. | • **Spatial Patch Tiling (Convolutional Kernel Mode)**: Use an $N_{\text{in}} = 4$ or $9$ input chip as a programmable $2\times 2$ or $3\times 3$ optical convolutional front-end scanning across $28\times 28$ (`MNIST`/`Fashion-MNIST`) or $64\times 64$ images (`Nature_21V589_44` Xu; `Nature_21V589_52` Feldmann).<br>• **Time-Multiplexed Block Decomposition**: Tile larger matrices into hardware-sized blocks (`Science_24V384_202` Taichi; `Nature_25V640_361` Hua PACE). |
| **Tier 2 (`T2`)**<br>*Nat. Photon.*, *Nat. Electron.*, *Nat. Mach. Intell.*, *Nat. Comput. Sci.* | **2–3 task families**:<br>1. Hardware calibration + matrix/operator fidelity characterization.<br>2. At least one hardware-in-the-loop classification task (`Iris`, `Digits`, `MNIST`, `Vowel`) **paired with** a higher-dimensional task (`Fashion-MNIST`, `CIFAR-10` features, audio recognition, or medical image segmentation). | • **Strict Train-Only Feature Compression**: Fit `PCA`, wavelet, or Fourier feature extraction **strictly on the training split** (`pca.fit(X_train)`), project `X_val` and `X_test` using the frozen training basis, and map the top $D \le N_{\text{in}}$ components to the optical input ports.<br>• **Multi-Port Class Mapping**: When mapping $K_{\text{out}}$ physical output ports to $C$ classes, specify the exact fixed port-grouping or linear readout projection matrix before evaluating test samples. |
| **Tier 3 (`T3`)**<br>*Nat. Commun.*, *Sci. Adv.* | **2 tasks + full ablation table**:<br>1. Device/core physical transfer matrix or spectrum characterization.<br>2. Two distinct ML or signal-processing tasks on hardware (plus hardware-calibrated numerical scaling to larger $N$ or harder datasets in `Fig. 5`). | • Clearly label which panels are **physical hardware measurements** vs. **hardware-calibrated numerical simulations** in the figure title, panel header, and caption (`"Hardware experiment"` vs. `"Simulation using measured noise/dispersion model"`). Never mix them silently! |
| **Tier 4 (`T4`)**<br>*LSA*, *eLight*, *Adv. Photon.*, *PhotoniX*, *LPR*, *Optica*, *PR*, *ACS Photon.* | **1–2 clean tasks with complete error bars**:<br>1. Thorough physical characterization ($Q$, ER, $V_\pi L$, switching energy, endurance, crosstalk).<br>2. One well-documented classification, edge-detection, or logic/computing demo with explicit digital baseline comparison. | • Report **exact integer numerator/denominator counts** alongside confusion matrices whose row sums equal the exact class sample counts. |

## 3. Strict ML Hygiene & Statistical Reporting Rules (Zero Data Leakage)

1. **Preprocessing & Normalization Boundary**:
   - All feature scalers (`MinMaxScaler`, `StandardScaler`), `PCA` projections, and voltage-mapping look-up tables must be fit **exclusively on the training split** (`X_train`), and frozen before transforming validation and test sets.
2. **Terminology Hygiene (`"Distillation"`, `"On-Chip Training"`, `"Non-Volatile"`)**:
   - **"Knowledge Distillation"**: Only use the word `"distillation"` when a trained teacher network explicitly supervises a student network via soft logits. Dimensionality reduction via `PCA` or feature selection is **compression / projection**, not distillation.
   - **"On-Chip / In-Situ Training"**: Specify whether gradients are computed via **optical wave interference** (`Science_23V380_398` Pai), **hardware-in-the-loop voltage perturbations** ($\mathbf{V} \pm \delta\mathbf{V}$ measured on the chip, updated on a host PC), or **off-chip digital surrogate simulation**.
   - **Small-Sample Integer Verification**: For any split with $n \le 200$ samples, every reported accuracy percentage $p\%$ must satisfy $\text{round}(p \cdot n / 100) / n \approx p / 100$, and confusion-matrix cells must sum to $n$.
